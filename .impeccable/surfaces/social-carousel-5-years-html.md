## Scope and mode

Social carousel artwork for Instagram and Facebook, authored as HTML at the publishing
canvas (1080 × 1350) and screenshotted to PNG. Mode: **Persuade** — the viewer should
believe the numbers are real, feel the partners deserve credit, and know a free trial
exists. Not part of the website; it borrows the website's visual system unchanged.

## Audience, job, action

Two audiences share one post. The existing six client organisations (and their own
audiences) must see themselves credited by name and logo. Prospective Canterbury
operators must see a proof asset no competitor can produce, then find one clear action.
The action is a free-trial enquiry via hungryworms.nz; the Uncle Bob's shop path is
deliberately absent from this post so the credit-giving does not turn into a sales pitch.

## Proof and content

Everything on a slide comes from the live Master Log via Supabase: 72,385.73 kg diverted,
180,964.33 kg CO₂e, 39.3427 cars, 1,488 individual pickups across 379 collection days.
The single approved marketing claim used is "99% of free-trial clients continue as
long-term partners". Logos are the six approved partners in PRODUCT.md.

## Constraints

- The company was registered 3 August 2021; the pickup log starts 3 March 2023. These two
  periods may never be presented as one. Every totals slide carries its data window.
- Aggregate figures only. Per-client weights or pickup counts are as forbidden here as in
  the portal.
- Kiln Orange appears exactly once per carousel, on the closing CTA.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

**"Weight made visible"** (surface-concept candidate 3; seed key b3551d9e), chosen by the
user from three rendered comps as "C base plus B's scoreboard slide". Every abstract
figure is drawn as countable units in the brand's own marks, and a part-unit is clipped to
its true fraction rather than rounded or faded.

The memorable moment is the pair of slides 2 and 3: 73 solid tonne-blocks, then 181 dashed
tonne-blocks at the identical size on an identically reserved field. The ×2.5 emission
factor is read as sheer quantity before any caption explains it. Slide 1's field of 1,488
dots (one per weighed pickup) opens the carousel and returns faint behind the closing
"Join them.", which turns the CTA into "room for one more" rather than a pitch.

Approved comps: `social/out/comps/C1.png`, `C2.png`, `C3.png` (structure), with
`social/out/comps/B2.png` supplying the scoreboard slide. Rejected: direction A, whose
chained arithmetic operators were too fine to survive a phone screen.
