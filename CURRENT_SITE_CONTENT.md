## Our Process page
- Headline: "Food Waste to Plant Food. Saving the Environment One Bite at a Time."
- Flow: Waste Collection → Natural Breakdown (worms create premium vermicast, no toxic byproducts) → Investing in our future (commercial machinery speeds composting) → Going full circle (worm castings → Uncle Bob's Vermicast Tonic → healthy plants → food back on plates).
- **Sustainability facts (use for credibility/education):**
  - ~**4% of total GHG emissions** come from food & organic waste.
  - For every **1 kg of food waste, >2.5 kg of CO₂** is emitted. (← this is the basis of our impact formula)
  - Food in landfill generates **methane, a GHG ~25× more potent than CO₂**.
  - Sources: NZ Ministry for the Environment (reducing food waste); UN FAO 2013 Food Wastage Footprint.
