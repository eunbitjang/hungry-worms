# Hungry Worms — Staff Portal & Pickup App
### A quick how-to (takes 2 minutes to learn)

Hi Mark — here's your new Staff Portal. It replaces the old Google Form: now you log a whole
client visit on **one screen** instead of submitting the form once per bin.

---

## 1. Getting in (first time only)

1. Open this link on your phone or computer:
   **https://hungry-worms.vercel.app/portal/staff**
2. You'll see a login screen. Tap **"Sign in with a password"**.
3. Enter:
   - **Email:** info@hungryworms.nz
   - **Password:** sent to you separately (never written in this guide — the code repo is public)
4. Tap **Sign In**.

That's it — your device now stays signed in, so next time it opens straight to the portal (no
login needed).

---

## 2. The Staff Portal

After signing in you'll see four tools:

- **📊 All-clients dashboard** — the impact numbers for every client in one place.
- **🚛 Log a pickup** — record a collection visit. *(This is the one you'll use most.)*
- **🔧 Fix a pickup** — correct or delete a pickup you've already logged.
- **🗑️ Clients & bins** — add a client or change what the pickup form shows for one.

Tap either card to open it.

---

## 3. How to log a pickup

1. Open **Log a pickup**.
2. The **date & time fill in automatically** (today). Logging an older visit from a memo? Tick
   **"Set a different date"** and choose the date.
3. Pick the **client** from the dropdown. The right bins for that client appear automatically.
4. Type the **weight in kg** into each bin you collected:
   - **Leave empty any bins you didn't collect** — only the boxes you fill get saved.
   - For some clients you'll also choose the **waste type**, **location**, or **bin size** from a
     small menu next to the box.
5. The **running total** at the bottom adds up as you go.
6. Add a **note** if you need to (optional).
7. Tap **Save**. You'll get a confirmation showing exactly what was logged.
8. Tap **"Log another pickup"** for the next client — or you're done!

> Every box is optional. Some days extra bins are full, some days an assigned bin is empty — just
> fill in whatever you actually collected.

---

## 4. Fixing a mistake

Typed 45 instead of 4.5, picked the wrong client, or saved the same visit twice? Fix it yourself:

1. Open **Fix a pickup** (or tap **"Fix it here"** on the "Pickup logged" screen).
2. Your recent pickups are listed newest first, one card per visit. Use the **Client** menu to
   narrow the list.
3. Tap **Edit** on the wrong line, change the weight, date, client, waste type, location, bin
   size or note, and tap **Save changes**.
4. Logged something that never happened (or twice)? Tap **Delete** and confirm.

The Google Sheet changes straight away and the website totals catch up about a minute later.
This screen needs you to be signed in, because it can change saved data.

> Only pickups logged with the app show up here. Entries from the old Google Form are still
> fixed directly in the Google Sheet.

---

## 5. Changing a client's bins (Clients & bins)

New client, an extra bin, a new waste type, or a client that stopped? Change it yourself:

1. Open **Clients & bins** (or tap **"Change them in Clients & bins"** under the bins on the
   pickup form).
2. Tap **Edit** next to the client, or **+ Add a client**.
3. Change what you need:
   - **Bins** — one row per bin you weigh. **+ Add a bin** copies the last one; **Remove** drops one.
   - **Waste type** — type one (e.g. `Food waste`), or several separated by commas
     (e.g. `Food waste, Yard / green waste`) to get a choice on the form.
   - **Also ask for** — a **Location** or **Bin size** choice, with the options separated by commas.
   - **Note** — a short reminder shown above the bins.
   - **Logo for the impact report** — paste a link to the client's logo image.
   - **Show this client in Log a pickup** — untick to hide a client who has stopped.
4. Tap **Save changes**. The pickup form uses it straight away.

Pickups you already logged are not changed. The **name saved in the sheet** is fixed once a
client is added, so all of their pickups stay together in the reports.

---

## 6. The all-clients dashboard

Open it any time to see:
- Total waste diverted, CO₂e avoided, cars off the road, and how many clients are active.
- A **per-client breakdown** (tap a client to filter the log to just them).
- Charts and a full collection log you can **export to CSV**.

---

## 7. Make it one tap (recommended)

So you don't have to type the link each time, add the portal to your phone's home screen:
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**
- **Android (Chrome):** tap the **⋮** menu → **Add to Home screen**

It'll look like an app icon and open straight to the portal. (Bookmark whichever screen you use
most — the portal, the pickup form, or the dashboard.)

---

## Good to know

- **One screen, all bins** — no more submitting once per bin.
- **You can't break anything.** If a weight is wrong, fix it under **Fix a pickup**.
- **You stay signed in** on your device, so it's quick every time.

Any questions or anything that feels off, just flag it. 🌱
