# Brand guide (for the new site)

## Identity
- **Name:** Hungry Worms (Hungry Worms Ltd). Sister product brand: **Uncle Bob's** (regenerative garden products, separate site unclebobs.co.nz).
- **Logo:** green "Hungry Worms Recycling" worm mark (currently on Wix). Ask the team for the final vector/PNG logo + any Uncle Bob's logo. Provide a placeholder slot in the header/footer until supplied.
- **Positioning line:** "BE PART OF THE SOLUTION NOT THE POLLUTION."
- **One-liner:** Canterbury's full-circle commercial food & green-waste recycling — diverting waste from landfill, cutting emissions, and turning it into premium NZ-made plant food.

## Voice & tone
- Warm, optimistic, credible, locally-proud (Aotearoa / Canterbury / Christchurch). Sustainability-led but **business-practical** — speak to ESG managers, GMs, ops leads who need measurable results, easy onboarding, and a trustworthy partner.
- Emphasise: **effortless** ("simple-as"), **measurable** (real numbers, reports), **local/closed-loop**, **proven** (named partners + a free trial with 99% continuation).
- Avoid greenwashing fluff — back claims with the live numbers and case studies.

## Colour direction
- Eco/natural palette — **greens** as primary (fresh leaf green + deep forest green), warm **earth/soil** neutrals, clean off-white backgrounds. A single warm accent (amber/terracotta) for CTAs is fine. Keep it flat, modern, high-contrast, accessible (WCAG AA).
- Suggested starting tokens (Claude Code may refine to match the real logo):
  - primary green `#1F8A4C`, deep green `#0F5132`, leaf/lime accent `#7FB800`, soil/ink `#2B2A26`, off-white `#F7F6F1`, warm CTA `#E07A2F`.
- Dark mode optional (nice for the portal). Charts: greens/teals for waste/CO₂e, a neutral for context.

## Typography
- Clean, friendly, modern sans for UI/body (e.g., Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans). A slightly characterful display face for big hero/stat numbers is welcome. Two weights, generous spacing.

## Imagery
- Real NZ/Canterbury photography: kitchens/cafés, bins, worms/vermicast, gardens/produce, partner sites. The "full circle" visual (food waste → worms → plant food → produce/retail) is a signature — lean into it.

- WCAG 2.1 AA, responsive (mobile-first), fast (good Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals), SEO meta per page, OG images. This is a sales asset — polish matters.
