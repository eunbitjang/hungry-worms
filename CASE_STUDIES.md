# Case studies & social proof (high priority for the new home page)

Hungry Worms' clients are well-known NZ organisations — **lead with them**. The home page needs a
prominent **partner logo strip** AND at least one rich, inspirational **case story**. These stories
are the strongest conversion tool: they make prospects want to try the service.

## Flagship story — Willowbank Wildlife Reserve (the "closed loop")
The signature, most inspirational story. Tell it visually as a full circle:
1. Willowbank generates food & green waste.
2. Hungry Worms collects and recycles it (compost worms) into **plant food / fertiliser** (Uncle Bob's).
3. Those products are **sold back in Willowbank's own souvenir/gift shop**.
4. → A true **full-circle / closed-loop sustainability** model: their waste returns to them (and their visitors) as a premium retail product.
- Why it matters: proves the model isn't just "waste removal" — it's regeneration + a new revenue/brand story for the client. Frame as "what closed-loop looks like in practice."

## Proven impact — Sudima Hotels
- Since 2024, across **Sudima Christchurch City + Christchurch Airport**, food-waste recycling has offset
  the GHG emissions of **Sudima's entire South Island vehicle fleet**.
- Testimonial — **Rajas Patil, Hotel General Manager, Sudima Christchurch Airport:**
  "Hungry Worms is making food waste recycling effortless, seamlessly integrating it into our operations.
  They are playing a key role in helping us achieve our ESG goals, including offsetting emissions from our
  vehicle fleet. More than a service provider, they are a true sustainability partner."
