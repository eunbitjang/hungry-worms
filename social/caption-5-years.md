## One rule for every caption on this post

The company is five years old. **The record is not.** It starts 3 March 2023 — not because
nothing was collected before then, but because that is when the team started weighing and
logging every pickup, having realised the number was the whole point. So:

- Never write "since our first pickup" or "since we started collecting". Both are false;
  there were collections before the log. Write **"since we started keeping records in
  March 2023"**.
- Never thank a named client for "five years", or imply their relationship ran that long.
  The longest is about 3.4 years, and these are people who will read it.
- Credit the company's age to the company, and every tonnage figure to the record period.
  Every slide carries its own data window for this reason.

## The trial: what may and may not be said

Hungry Worms offers a **one-month trial at the normal rate, with no long-term commitment** —
run it for a month, then carry on or wind it up, no exit fee. It is **not free**, and no
caption, slide or page may call it a free trial.

**The "99% of trial clients continue" figure is retired and must not be republished
anywhere.** Only one client has ever actually taken a trial; almost everyone else commits
after the first meeting without one. A percentage drawn from a single case is not a
statistic, and this brand's whole position is that its published numbers survive being
checked.

That the meeting alone usually closes it is a genuinely strong fact — but it has never been
counted, so write it as the plain observation it is ("most clients decide after one
conversation"), never as a percentage.
