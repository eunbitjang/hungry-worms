## Hungry Worms — Facebook

> Where does your food waste go? ♻️
>
> There's a simple order of what's best:
>
> 1️⃣ **Prevent** — waste less food in the first place
> 2️⃣ **Rescue** — get good surplus food to people who need it
> 3️⃣ **Recycle** — turn the scraps into something useful
> 4️⃣ **Recover** — energy only, so the nutrients are lost
> 5️⃣ **Landfill** — the last resort, where rotting food releases methane
>
> Hungry Worms works at the Recycle level. We collect the food scraps that can't be prevented or rescued, and our compost worms turn them into natural fertiliser for Canterbury soil. Every collection is weighed and reported, so our partners can see exactly what they've kept out of landfill. 🌱
>
